## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a calm, authoritative, and frictionless interface for a multi-stakeholder healthcare management ecosystem. It addresses clinical administrators, practitioners, and patients who demand absolute clarity, rapid scanability, and effortless navigation under high-stress conditions.

The visual style blends modern corporate minimalism with refined clinical ergonomics:
- **Atmosphere:** Controlled, sterile yet reassuring, surgical in alignment and whitespace distribution.
- **Hierarchy:** High-density data presentation without cognitive fatigue. Visual weight relies on deliberate contrast rather than ornamental decoration.
- **Restraint:** Complete avoidance of playful illustrations, skeletal skeuomorphism, or saturated multi-tone gradients. Utility, legibility, and patient safety govern every structural choice.

## Colors

The palette leverages deep navy to convey clinical competence and institutional trust, balanced by an active teal accent reserved for primary operational moments and vital sign visual cues.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary Navy (`#123B5D`):** Primary actions, active navigation states, foundational brand presence, and primary iconography.
- **Teal Accent (`#14B8A6`):** Focal interactive triggers (e.g., "Schedule Consultation", "Start Telehealth Call"), progress metrics, active toggles, and live status badges.
- **Canvas Base (`#F5F8FC`):** Cool ice-white background creating clear separation against content cards.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white surfaces for data cards, modals, tables, and side sheets.
- **Text Tiers:**
  - High Contrast Headings (`#172B4D`): Maximum readability for patient names, diagnoses, and vital metrics.
  - Secondary Slate (`#64748B`): Timestamps, medical record numbers, supporting captions, and table column headers.
- **Structure & Hairlines (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise 1px dividers, grid outlines, and table cell separations.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success / Cleared:** `#10B981` text & border on `#ECFDF5` background.
- **Warning / Pending Lab:** `#F59E0B` text & border on `#FFFBEB` background.
- **Destructive / Alert:** `#EF4444` text & border on `#FEF2F2` background.
- **Info / Telehealth Live:** `#0284C7` text & border on `#F0F9FF` background.

## Typography

The type system is powered entirely by Inter to maintain absolute visual consistency across clinical metrics, dense scheduling grids, and EHR narrative text.

### Implementation Guidelines
- **Tabular Figures (`tnum`):** Ensure OpenType tabular numbers are enabled for all clinical measurements, timestamps, vital signs, and currency values to maintain alignment in tables and metric arrays.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Apply `label-sm` with uppercase styling exclusively to section categorizers, status pills, and column keys.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Paragraph copy and clinical case histories strictly use `body-md` (14px) with 20px line heights to maintain dense but legible record reading.

## Layout & Spacing

The platform is designed around a responsive 1440px desktop canvas utilizing a 12-column fluid grid.

### Breakpoints & Structure
- **Desktop (1440px+):** Fixed persistent navigation rail (260px expanded / 72px icon-only), 12-column layout with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and 32px (`2rem`) page margins.
- **Tablet / Clinical Cart (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and margins; sidebar collapses to an overlay drawer.
- **Mobile Handheld (320px – 767px):** 4-column layout with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and margins; single-column vertical stack for patient summary cards, metric tiles, and schedules.

### Density & Rhythms
- Compact 4px base increments control internal component padding (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px).
- Data-heavy panels employ compact vertical padding (8px to 12px) to maximize above-the-fold visibility during triage review.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual separation relies on crisp boundary definitions and low-contrast surface contrast rather than dramatic vertical drop-shadows.

### Structural Levels
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Pure `#F5F8FC` ground plane.
- **Surface Panels & Metric Cards (Level 1):** `#FFFFFF` background with a mandatory 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and an ambient, low-diffusion shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04)`.
- **Dropdowns, Popovers & Context Menus (Level 2):** `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) and focused ambient depth: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.04)`.
- **Clinical Modals & Telehealth Drawers (Level 3):** `#FFFFFF` floating layer with deep containment shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(18, 59, 93, 0.06)`, anchored over a `#123B5D` backdrop overlay with 35% opacity.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture uses balanced medium rounding to soften clinical formality while sustaining a modern, technical structure.

### Radius System
- **Cards, Modals & Clinical Panels:** 12px (`0.75rem`) border radius, striking a balance between structure and modern ergonomics.
- **Interactive Controls (Inputs, Buttons, Dropdowns):** 8px (`0.5rem`) standard radius.
- **Pills, Badges & Toggles:** 9999px fully rounded boundaries for micro-status indicators, doctor specialization chips, and role tags.
- **Icon Containers & Metric Badges:** 8px (`0.5rem`) soft square enclosures.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Navy):** Solid `#123B5D` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Hover state shifts to `#0D2B44`. Focus ring: 2px offset with `#14B8A6`.
- **Vibrant Accent CTA (Teal):** Solid `#14B8A6` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Reserved for primary operational transitions (e.g., "Admit Patient", "Confirm Prescription"). Hover state shifts to `#0D9488`.
- **Secondary Outlined:** Pure white background, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, `#172B4D` text. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.
- **Destructive:** Solid `#EF4444` fill or `#FEF2F2` background with `#EF4444` text for irreversible clinical removals.

### Badges & Status Chips
- Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padded `4px 10px` with `label-sm` uppercase text.
- **Available / Stable:** `#10B981` text over `#ECFDF5`.
- **Pending / Lab Review:** `#D97706` text over `#FFFBEB`.
- **Critical / Emergency:** `#DC2626` text over `#FEF2F2`.
- **Telehealth / Sync:** `#0284C7` text over `#F0F9FF`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border 1px `#E2E8F0`, corner radius 8px (`0.5rem`).
- Height: 40px for regular entry; 32px for table-embedded quick filters.
- **Focus State:** 1px border in `#14B8A6` with an accompanying subtle teal glow: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.15)`.
- Placeholder text in `#94A3B8`.

### Clinical Cards & Metric Tiles
- Surface: `#FFFFFF` with 12px corner radius, 1px `#E2E8F0` border, and Level 1 ambient shadow.
- **Metric Tiles:** Feature a 40x40px icon enclosure tinted in primary or teal (10% opacity), a bold 32px tabular numerical reading, a micro sparkline trend indicator, and a secondary comparison delta label.

### Data Tables
- Header row styled with `#F8FAFC` background, 1px bottom border `#E2E8F0`, and text set to uppercase `label-md` in `#64748B`.
- Row height: 48px standard, 40px compact.
- Alternating hover row state using `#F8FAFC`. Active selected row indicated by a 3px vertical accent bar in `#14B8A6` on the far left cell edge.

### Specialty Components
- **Patient Banner:** Persistent, full-width high-contrast card at the top of medical records detailing allergies (in destructive tags), blood type, DOB, and primary physician.
- **Vital Sign Sparkline Tracker:** Clean single-stroke SVG line graphs (`#14B8A6` or `#123B5D`) with zero fill gradients, maintaining an ultra-clean clinical review state.
